What it does

Captions where people look, in the language they read

Put live captions over any meeting, lecture, video call or slide deck, offline on your own computer or translated for a multilingual audience.

Offline subtitles

Pick a Whisper model and one of 99 spoken languages, or let the app detect the language. Once a model is installed, recognition runs on your computer with no account, API key or internet connection, on its graphics processor when it has a suitable one.

Translation for your audience

Whisper translates into English offline. For other languages, translate with Gemini into 78 languages, with DeepL into 42 or with OpenAI into 13, using your own API key. Show two languages at once, or the original speech beneath the translation. If a cloud engine stops mid-event, one click carries on with offline Whisper in the same session.

On top of everything

Captions appear in a transparent overlay that stays on top of your slides, call or any other app, and lets clicks pass through to the window beneath. It reopens where you last placed it, remembered for each display setup.

Your choice of audio

Listen to a room microphone, the audio playing on your computer, a single app, or the microphone and system audio together.

Tuned for the room

Typeface, size, colours, backing and reading pace; Fit window, Compact or Stable reading, with right-to-left scripts. Save meeting profiles, and pause during breaks with a notice such as “Back soon” on the overlay.

A transcript you keep

Find words and add markers during a session. Copy the last 5, 10 or 30 minutes, and export text, Markdown, SRT or WebVTT. Optional local history saves sessions to search and reopen later.

Captions in Zoom

Share one caption language with a Zoom meeting's own caption bar, using the caption link the host copies in Zoom. Attendees turn the captions on in Zoom and install nothing.

Game voice chat

Follow voice chat in another language. The overlay is a separate window, not something injected into the game, so run the game in windowed or borderless mode.

Where the rates come from

Providers change their prices: check theirs before a long session. The app shows a running cost estimate, and an optional spending guard can warn you when it passes a limit you set.

  • Gemini and OpenAI: the Gemini API pricing and OpenAI API pricing pages, last checked 27 August 2026. Gemini is a range because its output is billed only while it translates; OpenAI's translation figure includes the transcript of the original speech, billed on top of the translation. OpenAI subtitles are $0.017 per minute, checked 23 November 2026.
  • Mistral: the Voxtral Mini Transcribe Realtime 3 model card, $0.008 per minute, checked 21 October 2026. Mistral lists the model as a public preview.
  • DeepL: not on DeepL's public pricing page, which sells speech hours inside its API plans. The figure is the overage price per hour of speech to text that third parties report, checked 11 November 2026.

Privacy

Your audio goes where you send it, and nowhere else

The developer operates no server, account system or telemetry for this app, and receives no audio, captions, transcripts, API keys or usage data from it.

Read the privacy policy
  • With Whisper, audio stays on your computer. Once a model is installed, captions need no network connection.
  • With a cloud engine, audio goes straight to the provider you chose. The first time you start a session with each provider, the app asks before sending it any audio.
  • API keys stay in Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain, and are sent only to their provider.
  • Transcripts are yours. They are written to disk only when you save them, or when you turn on history or crash recovery, which stay off until you enable them.
  • Sharing captions with Zoom starts only when you choose it, and sends caption text, never audio, directly to Zoom.
